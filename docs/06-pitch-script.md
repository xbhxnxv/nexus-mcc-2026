# Pitch script

The five-minute pitch delivered for the challenge, kept here as part of the record. It opens with the problem, justifies each financial decision against a source, and closes with a clear ask, in line with the Turner & Townsend brief.

---

**Cover (0:00–0:20).** NEXUS. Net Zero Energy Transition Exchange at Tyseley. £50 million. 6,000 square metres. 42 months to delivery. This is our proposal, and in five minutes we'll show you why Birmingham needs it and why now.

**The problem (0:20–0:55).** Birmingham already has the infrastructure. A 10-megawatt biomass plant. The UK's largest green hydrogen refuelling station. The Birmingham Energy Innovation Centre. The National Centre for Decarbonisation of Heat opening this year. But these assets operate in isolation. The university produces world-class research with no pilot or demonstration space to move ideas into deployment. And East Birmingham, one of England's most deprived areas, sits next to all of this investment with fragmented community benefit. The infrastructure exists. The translation layer doesn't.

**The opportunity (0:55–1:25).** Tyseley Energy Park is already one of the UK's most active clean-energy clusters. Live energy infrastructure, an existing university partnership, a brownfield site of 8,692 square metres, and a neighbour project completing this year. We're not building in a vacuum. We're plugging into a live ecosystem.

**The solution (1:25–2:05).** NEXUS is a single integrated 6,000 square metre, three-storey facility around three research pillars. BECCS, a pilot-scale carbon capture rig tied directly to the live biomass plant, the first of its kind in UK academia. Energy storage, with next-generation battery chemistry, green hydrogen electrolysis and vehicle-to-grid testing on the park's existing infrastructure. And sustainable computing, with immersion-cooled HPC, neuromorphic computing and carbon-aware algorithm research.

**Finances (2:05–2:50).** The finances are tight but deliverable. We benchmarked against BCIS 2026 data for UK university laboratories, a range of £4,000 to £6,500 per square metre. Our construction rate reflects the specialist lab and MEP requirements, and the all-in project cost, including fees, risk and a 3.5% inflation provision from Bank of England CPIH data, comes to £50 million. We use NEC4 Option C for the research pillars, a target-cost contract that shares risk, and Option A for enabling works, procured through the Constructing West Midlands framework, the same route the university used for its £46.5 million School of Engineering.

**Revenue and ROI (2:50–3:20).** NEXUS is a platform for income, not just a cost. Research grants from UKRI, industry memberships across three tiers, demonstrator access, residency, training and events. Set against operating cost, the facility is designed to reach a self-sustaining position, with a working capital reserve held inside the capital budget to bridge the ramp-up.

**Stakeholders and sustainability (3:20–3:50).** We mapped stakeholders on Mendelow's power-interest matrix. University leadership, the city council, WMCA and Tyseley partners are managed closely. Funding bodies and emergency services are kept satisfied through formal reporting. Sustainability follows all four APM pillars, from BREEAM Excellent and on-site renewables to a 20% local construction workforce and annual public impact reporting.

**Timeline (3:50–4:20).** Delivery is phased across 42 months. Feasibility and surveys in 2026 de-risk the brownfield site before any money is committed. Planning submission in early 2027. Main construction through 2027 to 2028. Specialist fit-out in late 2028. NEXUS opens in 2029.

**Risks (4:20–4:45).** Five key risks. The BECCS pilot integration is the highest, so our mitigation is NEC4 Option C and a pre-feasibility study before Stage 2. Brownfield conditions are addressed through early ground investigation. The trades shortage is managed through early contractor engagement and locked rates at Stage 3. Technology obsolescence is avoided by phasing equipment to Year 3. And if funding falls behind, the working capital reserve provides the buffer.

**Close (4:45–5:00).** NEXUS is not just a building. It is Birmingham's platform for deployable net-zero solutions. We're asking you to approve NEXUS and build the missing link.
